WHY IT MATTERS

Why server-side tracking?

Browsers and ad blockers can limit tracking data. Server-side tracking helps collect and share more reliable conversion data with your marketing platforms.

01Recover Lost Conversion Data

Browsers, ad blockers, and privacy restrictions can sometimes prevent tracking events from being recorded properly. Server-side tracking helps reduce these data gaps by sending conversion events directly from your server to advertising platforms, making your tracking setup more reliable.

02Better Ad Optimization

Advertising platforms rely on conversion data to understand which users are taking valuable actions. With more reliable event data, platforms like Meta and Google can better understand campaign performance and use those signals to improve ad delivery and optimization.

03More Control Over Your Data

Server-side tracking gives you greater control over how website data is collected, processed, and shared with third-party platforms. You can manage the data sent to each platform and build a tracking setup that better fits your business needs and privacy requirements.

04Improved Data Accuracy

Combining browser-side and server-side tracking helps capture conversion events through two different methods. With proper event deduplication, duplicate events can be identified and handled correctly, helping maintain more consistent and accurate conversion reporting.

Server-side tracking still follows user consent and privacy rules, and needs deduplication so one sale is never counted twice. Both are part of how I set it up.

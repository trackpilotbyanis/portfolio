WHY IT MATTERS

Why server-side tracking?

Browsers now block or limit a lot of tracking data. Sending events from your server puts that data back under your control.

01Recover lost conversions

Ad blockers, Safari and iOS limits stop browser pixels from reporting some sales. Events sent from the server are not affected by these browser limits.

02Better ad optimization

Meta and Google learn from the conversions they receive. Fuller, cleaner data helps them find the right buyers and reduces wasted spend.

03Control over your data

You decide which data goes to which platform, and it can be sent through your own domain.

04Faster pages

Moving tags off the browser means fewer scripts for your visitors to load, so pages open faster.

Server-side tracking still follows user consent and privacy rules, and needs deduplication so one sale is never counted twice. Both are part of how I set it up.
